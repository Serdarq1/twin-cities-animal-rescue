About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2012, when three neighbors in south Minneapolis started fostering stray cats in their own homes. Word spread, and within two years a network of volunteers was caring for dogs, cats, and small animals from both cities.

Today we run a small adoption center, support more than one hundred foster homes, and help hundreds of pets find families every year. We are still powered almost entirely by volunteers.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: Every animal is treated with patience and respect, no matter its age or history.
  • Transparency: We share how donations are spent and report our outcomes every year.
  • Community: Rescue works best when neighbors, businesses, and volunteers work together.
  • Responsibility: We match pets and families carefully so adoptions last a lifetime.
