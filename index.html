Every Pet Deserves a Safe Place to Land

Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue gives homeless, surrendered, and at-risk animals a second chance. We provide medical care, foster homes, and careful adoption matching so every pet leaves us healthy and goes to a family that fits.

Our Impact in 2025

  • 612 dogs, cats, and small animals adopted into new homes
  • 148 active foster families across the metro area
  • 9,400 volunteer hours donated
  • 1,050 spay and neuter surgeries funded

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Meet pets who are ready for a home. Learn how adoption works.
  • Foster: Open your home for a few weeks so a pet can heal and grow. Read about fostering.
  • Volunteer: Walk dogs, socialize cats, or help at events. See volunteer roles.
  • Partner or donate: Local businesses and donors keep our doors open. Meet our partners.
